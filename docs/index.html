Why this matters

Three ideas behind the opportunity.

01
AI changes how people buy.
Intent replaces navigation. Instead of browsing, searching and comparing, users say what they want, and the assistant removes the friction, the decisions and the taps between them and done.
02
Rappi can own intelligent commerce.
One assistant already connects restaurants, Turbo, supermarkets and pharmacy. Few companies have this density of merchants, products and real-time logistics in a single app to orchestrate on the user's behalf.
03
This unlocks new value.
Faster task completion and cross-vertical orchestration create levers across conversion, frequency, order value and retention, turning a super-app into an AI commerce platform.
